## 1. Executive Summary: The "Page 2 Validation Gate" Rule

### The Strategy Verified (Why This is Optimal SEO):
* **On-Page SEO (Relevance) gets you to Page 2 / Top of Page 3 (Positions 11–26):** Through keyword targeting, H2 optimization, content depth, schema, and internal linking, Google evaluates your topical relevance.
* **External Backlinks (Authority) push you from Page 2 into Top 5:** Buying backlinks for keywords that Google hasn't even ranked on Page 2 or 3 is a waste of budget. Google dampens links to pages that lack indexing history or topical trust.
* **The Rule of Thumb:**  
  > **"Never spend backlink budget on a keyword unless Google has already organically validated the page by ranking it on Page 2 or top/middle of Page 3 (Positions 10.0 to 26.5)."**
* **The Action:** We remove unranked keywords (like `office cleaning perth`) from the paid backlink blueprint. We incubate them organically first. **100% of our 9 available backlink signals are concentrated exclusively on high-impression queries currently sitting in the Page 2 / Page 3 sweet spot.**
